Our Story

Susanto Liman founded MR.COFFEE after leaving his company job, seeking income but discovering along the way that coffee tells a story. MR.COFFEE was born from passion and perseverance.

Vision

To brew more than coffee — to brew stories, connections, and comfort in every cup served.

Mission

  • Deliver high-quality coffee with heart and story.
  • Create a cozy environment that connects people.
  • Support local farmers and sustainable sourcing.
